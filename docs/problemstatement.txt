## Problem Statement

Myntra has millions of users who add fashion products to their wishlist, indicating a strong initial interest but not necessarily a purchase intent. A significant portion of these wishlisted products never converts into a purchase within 30 days.

The key challenge is that Myntra does not have a scalable way to understand **why users hesitate after expressing interest in a product**.

User feedback is fragmented across App Store and Play Store reviews, Reddit discussions, social media, YouTube comments, fashion communities, and product reviews/Q&A. Manually analyzing these conversations is time-consuming and makes it difficult to identify recurring purchase barriers, quantify their prevalence, and understand how these barriers differ across user segments.

### Objective

Build an **AI-powered review and conversation discovery engine for Myntra** that automatically collects, cleans, categorizes, and analyzes publicly available user feedback to identify the key reasons why users:

* Add products to their wishlist
* Delay or abandon a purchase
* Compare shortlisted products
* Seek information outside Myntra
* Remain uncertain about fit, size, quality, styling, price, occasion, reviews, or social validation

The system should go beyond sentiment analysis and surface **actionable unmet needs and recurring purchase barriers** that could potentially improve Myntra's **wishlist-to-purchase conversion within 30 days**, without relying on monetary incentives.

### Core Question

> **“What unresolved user problems prevent a high-intent Myntra user from converting a wishlisted product into a purchase?”**

The output of the discovery engine should help the Product Manager **identify, quantify, prioritize, and compare these problems**, ultimately revealing the highest-impact opportunity areas for improving wishlist-to-purchase conversion.
